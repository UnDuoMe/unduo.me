# @-/me.unduo.components.home

The website's home page. `startHomePage()` builds it in the page's `#root` as a landing page. Its hero fills most of the first screen, centered: the name, a one-line description, "UnDuo is a secure, single file that you can audit and use locally.", and a card holding "Select your university below to get started:" and a searchable dropdown (placeholder "Search for your university...", label "Select Your University"), with a footer at the bottom: contact@unduo.me as an email link (`mailto:`), and GitHub, with its mark, linking to the source. Typing in the field filters the institutions under it, and choosing one with Enter or a click opens a modal dialog for it that shows one step at a time, with Back from step 2 on. Escape, the close button and Done on the last step close it, and focus returns to the field. It has no stepper; the institution's setup file keeps its own.

Under the hero (`home-landing.ts`):

- **Fully safe, but don't take our word for it.**: the user's heading and lead (you can audit UnDuo with all the major AIs: ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini; all three classify it as fully safe), then a carousel that shows one AI at a time, with arrows that wrap around, a button per AI and a sideways swipe; the slides out of view are `inert`. Each slide has its strongest model and its verdict ("Safe: yes") beside a screenshot of the conversation, which sits on the left from 960 px up and on top below that: the University of Toronto file and the prompt above the reply, which opens larger in a dialog. Beside it is the whole reply, from `aiReplies` in `ai-replies.ts`, as Markdown that `replyText()` turns into paragraphs, headings, lists and bold, all text nodes. It shows its first lines, fading out, until the chevron at the bottom of the card opens it in a box that scrolls; the chevron closes it again. The screenshots are `ai-reply-<id>.webp` in `apps/unduo-web/src/assets/`, taken by `vp run capture:ai` ([`configs/ai-replies`](../../configs/ai-replies)). The build publishes them with the other assets and `homeMount()` (the `./server` entry) puts them in an `#ai-reply-images` template with their SHA-256; `showScreenshot()` fetches each only if its hash matches and shows it as a blob: URL, as the setup file does with its screenshots.
- **How UnDuo Works**: the three steps, stacked as cards in the layout of Discord's landing page: an illustration on one side (alternating sides from 960 px up, above the text below that) and, on the other, "Step N", the step's line as a heading and a short description. The illustrations, in `how-art.ts`, are inline SVG drawn with `rect`, `path` and `svgText` from `@-/me.unduo.lib.dom`, hidden from screen readers, so the page fetches nothing for them; their QR codes are drawings that encode nothing.
- **Get Started**, a button at the bottom that jumps straight back up to the search, without smooth scrolling, focuses it and opens its list.

1. **Download**: "Download UnDuo's webpage, _but don't open it yet:_", the first words an underlined link (not a button) that saves `UnDuo - <institution name>.html` from `./files/<WHED number>`, then "Next, we'll show you how to verify it's safe to use." Beside it, a drawing (`downloadArt()`) shows a click on the download button and the browser's Downloads panel popping up under its button with the file.
2. **Verify**: drag the file into an AI and copy the prompt. A tab group picks the AI (ChatGPT, selected first, Claude, Gemini or Other), and a drawing beside the text shows a browser with its Downloads panel open over that AI's start page, the file dragged into its message box (`dragArt()` in `how-art.ts`, animated unless motion is reduced). The text reads "Drag and drop UnDuo's file into <AI> and paste in this prompt:" over the prompt. Under it, Copy prompt and a link that opens the chosen AI in a new tab, with an icon that says so; Other has no link and says "your favorite AI".
3. **Continue**: open the file once the AI gives the thumbs up. It has its own stepper from there. The page's closing advice follows. Beside it, a drawing (`openArt()`) shows a double click on the file in the Downloads panel opening it in a window of its own. Every step's drawing is inline SVG in `how-art.ts`, animated unless motion is reduced, and the dialog widens to 56rem from 900 px to fit it beside the text.

`?university=` holds the institution whose dialog is open, as an id or an unambiguous name, and `#step=` the step, so Back, Forward, reload and shared links work. A link with an institution but no step opens the download step, and closing the dialog drops both from the address. An unpublished institution opens no dialog. Step names from earlier versions of the page (`university`, `overview`, `check`, `open`, `safe`) still lead to their new steps.

The prompt, `aiPrompt` in `ai-prompt.ts`, lives in this page's script, which the file doesn't contain, so the file an AI reads doesn't hold the question put to that AI. A test enforces that. It touches no DOM, so the package's `./server` entry exports it for scripts: `configs/ai-replies` asks ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude the same question and records their replies with screenshots.
